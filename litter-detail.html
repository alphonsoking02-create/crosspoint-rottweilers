The Decision

Why This Pairing

This breeding was selected to bring together Hercules' commanding structure and bone density with Mila's proven production quality and consistent temperament. Pedigree analysis shows strong compatibility in type, angulation, and working character.

The goal is to produce puppies that carry the Crosspoint standard forward — powerful, correct, and confident. This combination has been evaluated for genetic compatibility and structural complement, and represents one of the most intentional pairings in our current program cycle.

Our Standard

Raising the Standard

From birth, every Crosspoint puppy is raised under a structured development protocol. This includes early neurological stimulation, progressive socialization exposure, environmental enrichment, and a premium nutrition program anchored by Inukshuk Professional Dog Food. Our goal is to produce puppies that are not only well-bred — but well-started.

The Process

Selective Placement

Crosspoint litters are placed selectively. We evaluate every inquiry based on experience, lifestyle, environment, and intended purpose. Not every inquiry results in a placement — and we believe that's how it should be.

If this pairing aligns with what you're looking for, we encourage you to submit an inquiry early. We work through our waitlist thoughtfully, matching individual puppies with the homes best suited to bring out their potential.
