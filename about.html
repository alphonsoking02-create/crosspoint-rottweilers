The Crosspoint Program

The Program

A breeding program built on discipline, intention, and an unwillingness to compromise.

Philosophy

Why We Breed

Great dogs are not accidents. They are the result of intention, discipline, and an unwillingness to cut corners. Crosspoint Rottweilers was built on this principle — that every pairing should be justified, every decision should serve the breed, and every dog should carry the standard forward.

We are not interested in producing the most dogs. We are interested in producing the right dogs. That means careful pedigree analysis, honest evaluation of our own stock, and the patience to wait for the right pairing rather than settling for the convenient one.

This is a program with a long-term vision. Each generation is built on the one before it — structure refined, temperament proven, bloodlines deepened. The goal has never been volume. The goal has always been legacy.

Our Focus

What We Breed For

Structure. Temperament. Type. These three pillars define every breeding decision at Crosspoint.

We evaluate our dogs against the breed standard with clear eyes — not as owners, but as breeders with a responsibility to the next generation. Correct angulation, strong bone, balanced movement, and proper proportion are non-negotiable. But a beautiful dog with a poor temperament has no place in our program.

Temperament is assessed through real-world exposure, environmental testing, and honest observation. Our dogs must be confident, stable, and sound in character. This is what allows a Rottweiler to be both a serious working animal and a trusted family companion.

Development

How We Raise

Every puppy born at Crosspoint enters a structured development program from day one.

Neurological Stimulation

Bio-sensor exercises during the critical neonatal period to support neurological development and stress tolerance.

Progressive Socialization

Controlled exposure to surfaces, sounds, environments, and people at developmentally appropriate stages.

Environmental Enrichment

Novel stimuli, problem-solving opportunities, and confidence-building exercises throughout the critical development window.

Individual Assessment

Each puppy is evaluated for structure, drive, temperament, and placement suitability before matching with a home.

Standards

Nutrition & Care

The quality of what goes into a dog matters as much as the quality of the breeding behind it. At Crosspoint, our nutrition program is built around Inukshuk Professional Dog Food — a high-calorie, nutrient-dense formula designed for dogs that perform at the highest level.

We chose Inukshuk because it aligns with how we approach everything else: seriously, with research, and without compromise. Premium nutrition is one component of a broader commitment to raising dogs that are structurally sound, physically developed, and ready to thrive.

Combined with structured exercise, veterinary oversight, and environmental enrichment, our care program supports every stage of development — from whelping through placement and beyond.

Health

Tested. Verified. Transparent.

Every dog in the Crosspoint program undergoes comprehensive health screening before entering breeding consideration.

Orthopedic

OFA hip and elbow evaluations on all breeding dogs. Results documented and available.

Cardiac & Eyes

Cardiac clearance and ophthalmologic examination by board-certified specialists.

Genetic Testing

DNA panels including JLPP, DM, and breed-relevant genetic markers. Full transparency.

Beyond Placement

Our Commitment to You

When you bring a Crosspoint Rottweiler into your home, you are not just getting a dog — you are joining a program. We remain available for guidance on nutrition, training, health, and development long after placement day. Our goal is to produce dogs that represent Crosspoint well, and to support the people who raise them.
