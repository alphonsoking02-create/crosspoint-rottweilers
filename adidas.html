Bloodline

Pedigree Highlights

Adidas was bred by Ruben Rodriguez and is sired by Wyatt von der Obersten out of Britney von der Alten Festong — a pedigree built on Tannenfeld, Hause Edelstein, and Serbian/Croatian (SRB/CRO) bloodlines.

His sire, Wyatt, traces heavily to Doctor Timit-Tor through the Tannenfeld line, concentrating the substance, bone, and head type the Crosspoint program is built around. Wyatt is also the sire of our foundation female, Mila — closely tying Adidas to the program's female line.

Evaluation

Structure & Temperament

Structure

Adidas shows heavy bone and substance throughout, with a strong topline, correct rear angulation, and balanced proportions. Chest is deep and well-developed, shoulder layback is correct, and movement under load is powerful and efficient.

His head is masculine and typey — strong underjaw, correct stop, clean cheek, and dark pigmentation. He presents the kind of overall balance the Crosspoint program is selected for.

Temperament

Confident, composed, and stable. Adidas demonstrates the natural self-assurance expected of the breed — alert without reactivity, appropriate with people, and clear-headed under pressure. His character is one of his strongest traits and a primary reason he's in the program.

He transmits temperament consistency to his offspring, which is the hallmark of a reliable producing dog.

Program Role

Why Adidas

Adidas was selected for his ability to bring substance and head type to the pairing table without sacrificing balance or movement. He complements the female line by reinforcing bone, underjaw, and presence — the characteristics that define a Crosspoint-bred Rottweiler.

His pedigree depth gives pairing flexibility, and his documented health clearances provide the genetic confidence required for responsible breeding decisions.
