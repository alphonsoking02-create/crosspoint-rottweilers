Pedigree Highlights

CrossPoints Mila was bred by Richard Scott Mackenzie, sired by Wyatt von der Obersten and out of Kiki Bella von der Obersten.

Her maternal grandsire is CH. Rumpi “The Bear” Vom Beni — the same imported European male whose frozen semen will be paired back to her. That direct descent is what makes the planned breeding a purposeful line breeding, concentrating Rumpi's size, bone, structure, and head type in the next generation.

Evaluation

Structure & Temperament

Structure

Mila presents with strong bone, balanced proportions, and a feminine but powerful build. Her topline is correct, her angulation balanced, and her movement efficient. Her substance and head type align with the standard the program is built upon.

Her pigmentation is rich and her overall type is what we look for in a foundation female.

Temperament

Confident, stable, and engaged. Mila demonstrates the natural assurance expected of the breed — alert, biddable, and clear-headed under pressure. She is an exceptional dam who passes her clarity and composure to her puppies.

Her temperament consistency is one of her most important contributions to the program.
