How It Works

Reserving Your Puppy

Reservations at Crosspoint are handled personally. When a pairing is right for you, we arrange your deposit and placement directly with you — one conversation, no impersonal online checkout.

Submit an inquiry to start the conversation. We'll walk you through availability, pricing, the deposit, and pickup or transport, and answer any questions before anything is finalized.

The Reservation Process

Step 1 — Reach Out

Submit an inquiry through our contact form and tell us what you're looking for.

Step 2 — Arrange Your Deposit

Once we've connected, we arrange your deposit directly to hold your place on the list.

Step 3 — Placement

We coordinate the balance, pickup, or transport with you personally when your puppy is ready.

What's Included

Every puppy leaves Crosspoint with the foundation already in place.

Health Records

Up-to-date vaccinations, deworming schedule, and a vet-issued health certificate.

AKC Registration

Full registration paperwork and a verified pedigree of both sire and dam.

Starter Pack

Inukshuk Professional starter food, scent blanket, and a transition guide.

Lifetime Support

Direct access to us for the life of the dog. Questions, training, anything.
