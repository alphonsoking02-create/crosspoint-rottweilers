Our Process

Selective Placement

Crosspoint litters are placed — not sold. We evaluate every inquiry based on experience, lifestyle, environment, and intended purpose. Our goal is to match each puppy with the home best suited to support its potential. Not every inquiry results in a placement, and we believe that's how it should be.
