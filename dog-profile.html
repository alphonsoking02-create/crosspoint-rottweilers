Evaluation

Structure & Temperament

Structure

Hercules presents with excellent bone density and substance, a strong topline, correct rear angulation, and balanced proportions throughout. His chest is deep and well-developed with proper rib spring. Movement is powerful and efficient with strong drive from the rear and good reach in front.

His head is typey and masculine with correct stop, strong underjaw, and dark eye color. Overall, he embodies the structural standard that defines the Crosspoint program.

Temperament

Confident, stable, and composed. Hercules demonstrates the natural assurance expected of the breed — alert without reactivity, protective without aggression, and social with appropriate environmental awareness. His character is one of his strongest traits and a primary reason for his selection as a foundation male.

He passes his temperament consistency to his offspring, which is the hallmark of a reliable producing dog.

Program Role

Why Hercules

Hercules was selected as a foundation male for his ability to consistently produce offspring that carry his structural strengths and temperament stability. His pedigree depth allows for strategic pairing flexibility, and his documented health clearances provide the genetic confidence required for responsible breeding decisions.
